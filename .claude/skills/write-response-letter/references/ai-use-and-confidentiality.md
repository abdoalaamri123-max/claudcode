# AI Use, Disclosure, and Confidentiality

Apply this gate whenever AI processes unpublished submission materials.

## Confidentiality first

- Process only materials the user is authorized to provide.
- Do not place confidential manuscripts, reviews, decision letters, editor communications, data, or analyses in public skill repositories or public examples.
- Do not use materials obtained through an editor or reviewer role as training examples without explicit authorization and adequate de-identification.
- Prefer authorized, de-identified, synthetic, or publicly released examples.
- Warn the user before sending unpublished content to any third-party system whose retention, training, or confidentiality terms are unknown or unsuitable.

## Record actual AI use

Track whether AI was used for:

- organizing comments or detecting overlaps;
- inferring underlying concerns;
- revision strategy or theoretical reasoning;
- drafting or editing response text;
- literature search or citation work;
- code, data preparation, analysis, or interpretation;
- tables, figures, or presentation of results.

Do not describe substantive drafting or reasoning merely as "language editing."

## Check current disclosure rules

Before submission, consult the decision letter and the journal's current policy. Policies change, and rules written here do not override current official instructions.

- AOM currently requires disclosure of AI use by research stage, including writing and editing, and requires journal disclosures in the cover letter and acknowledgements.
- INFORMS currently permits generative AI in manuscript preparation but requires author responsibility and acknowledgement of the tool and nature of assistance.
- ASQ currently asks authors to disclose AI use and emphasizes that AI must not replace scholarly reading, analysis, writing, or judgment.
- APA currently requires disclosure and citation when generative AI is used in drafting an APA manuscript. Public guidance may not expressly classify every response letter use; flag ambiguity rather than claiming that disclosure is unnecessary.

## Produce a compliance note, not an automatic disclosure

Report:

1. the tool and model, if known;
2. the tasks for which it was used;
3. the authors' verification and responsibility;
4. the current policy source and date checked;
5. the possible disclosure location;
6. any unresolved question requiring author or editor confirmation.

Offer suggested disclosure language, but require author confirmation before inserting or submitting it.
