# 11 · 高频英文句式库 + 真实修回骨架

> 直接可用的地道句式，按功能分类。完整速查见 `templates/phrase-bank.md`。**句式是脚手架，具体内容必须贴合你的稿子。**

## 一、致谢开场
- "We thank the Editor and reviewers for their careful reading and constructive comments."
- "We are grateful for the opportunity to revise our manuscript."
- "The comments have substantially improved the paper."

## 二、每条开头的感谢
- "We thank the reviewer for this insightful/valuable/thoughtful comment."
- "We appreciate the reviewer raising this important point."
- "This is an excellent suggestion."

## 三、同意并修改
- "We agree and have revised accordingly (p.x, ll.y–z)."
- "Following the reviewer's suggestion, we have added…"
- "We have now clarified/expanded/rephrased…"
- "The results remain robust after…"

## 四、部分同意
- "We agree with the first point; regarding the second, we have taken a different approach because…"
- "We have partially addressed this by…"

## 五、礼貌反驳
- "We understand the concern and offer the following clarification."
- "We respectfully suggest that, given [reason], the current approach is appropriate, while remaining open to the editor's guidance."
- "A full analysis of X is beyond the scope of this study; we now note it as future work (p.x)."
- "Our original phrasing may have been unclear; we did not intend to claim X. We have rewritten this passage (p.x)."

## 六、承认局限
- "We agree this is a limitation and now discuss it explicitly (p.x)."
- "We have clarified the scope conditions of our claims."

## 七、冲突审稿人
- "Reviewer 1 suggested X while Reviewer 2 suggested Y; we have balanced these by…"
- "Should the editor prefer a different balance, we are happy to adjust."

## 八、结尾
- "We hope the revised manuscript addresses all concerns and is now suitable for publication."
- "We thank the reviewers again for helping us improve the paper."

## 真实修回骨架案例（体例示范）

### 案例：一封 major revision 回应信的骨架
```
Dear Editor,
[cover letter：致谢 + 三项主要改进 + 附逐条回应]

Response to Reviewers

Response to the Editor
E.1  [编辑硬要求] → 三段式回应

Reviewer 1
R1.1 [意见照抄] → Response: 感谢+同意+改动 → Revised (p.x, ll.)
R1.2 [意见] → Response: 部分同意，说明 → Revised (p.x)
R1.3 [意见=误解] → Response: 揽责澄清 → Revised (p.x)

Reviewer 2
R2.1 [补分析要求] → Response: 已补，报稳健性 → 新表3 (p.x)
R2.2 [与R1.4冲突] → Response: 指出冲突+权衡+交编辑
R2.3 [语言] → Response: 已专业润色

[结尾致谢]
```

> 用 `templates/response-letter-template.md` 落地这个骨架，用 `templates/point-by-point-table.md` 先把清单做全。
