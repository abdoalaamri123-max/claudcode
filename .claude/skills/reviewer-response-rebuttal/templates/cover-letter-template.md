# 修回 Cover Letter 模板（给编辑）

> 简短（半页–一页），突出主要改进。别复述逐条内容。配套见 `references/09`。

---

Dear Dr. [Editor Name],

Re: Manuscript #[ID], "[Title]"

Thank you for the opportunity to revise our manuscript. We are grateful to you and the [N] reviewers for the constructive feedback, which has substantially improved the paper.

We have carefully addressed **all** comments. A detailed point-by-point response accompanies this letter, and all changes are highlighted in the revised manuscript. The most important revisions include:

1. **[大改进1]** —— [一句话说明，如"新增稳健性检验，结论保持稳健（新表3）"]
2. **[大改进2]** —— […]
3. **[大改进3]** —— […]

[可选：若有重要意见未完全照做]
Regarding [某意见], we have [替代性处理] rather than [原要求], because [简要理由]; we would of course welcome your guidance on this point.

We believe the manuscript is now considerably stronger and hope it is suitable for publication in [Journal]. We look forward to your feedback.

Sincerely,
[Corresponding Author, on behalf of all authors]
[Affiliation, Email]

---

## 自检
- [ ] 半页到一页，简短
- [ ] 只抓 2–4 项主要改进，不逐条复述
- [ ] "addressed all comments" 属实
- [ ] 附了逐条回应信与标注修改稿
- [ ] 无错别字、语气专业
