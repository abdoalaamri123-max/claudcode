# Editorial Policy Checklist — Field Inventory

Source: Nature Portfolio `nr-editorial-policy-checklist` v2 (XFA form, April 2023 revision).
This form ensures compliance with editorial policies on research ethics and reproducibility.
"All relevant questions on the form must be answered." Walk every field.

## 1. Header
- **Corresponding author(s)** — free text.
- **Last updated by author(s)** — date `YYYY-MM-DD` (author fills).

## 2. Competing interests
Policy: authors must declare any competing financial and/or non-financial interest related to the work.
Pick one:
- "None of the authors have competing financial or non-financial interests," **OR**
- "One or more authors have a competing interest."
Evidence to look for: a "Competing interests" / "Conflict of interest" / "Declarations" section. Report which declaration the manuscript supports, and if it is missing.

## 3. Authorship
- **Authorship policy compliance** — confirm all listed authors agreed to contents, author list/order, and contribution statements. `👤 Author-only` confirmation, but flag if there is no author-contributions statement in the manuscript.
- **LLM confirmation** — confirm the author list does not include any Large Language Model. Note: any LLM use should be documented in Methods. Check whether the manuscript discloses LLM/AI-assistance and whether an LLM is (incorrectly) listed as an author.
- **Inclusion & Ethics statement** — encouraged where relevant (global-research collaborations). Check if present.

Evidence to look for: "Author contributions" heading; "CRediT"; any AI/LLM-use disclosure; "Inclusion & Ethics" / "global research" statement.

## 4. Data availability
Policy: every manuscript must include a data availability statement (accession codes / identifiers / links for public datasets; restrictions; clinical/third-party policy adherence).
- **Data availability statement** — confirm a full statement is in the manuscript; assess completeness.
- **Mandated accession codes (where applicable)** — choose one:
  - "All relevant accession codes are provided," **OR**
  - "Accession codes will be available before publication," **OR**
  - "No data with mandated deposition."
Evidence to look for: "Data availability" heading; **proteomics: PRIDE / ProteomeXchange PXD accessions, MassIVE, jPOST**; sequencing: GEO/SRA; structures: PDB/EMDB; generic: Zenodo/Figshare DOI. Determine whether mandated-deposition data types are present and whether codes are given.

## 5. Code availability
Policy: for any study using custom code or a mathematical algorithm central to the conclusions, a "Code availability" statement is required (a separate section after Data availability, before References), describing access and any restrictions.
- **Code availability statement** — confirm present and complete.
Evidence to look for: "Code availability" heading; GitHub/GitLab/Zenodo links; version/DOI; license; access restrictions. If the study has custom code (scripts, pipelines, an extension/plugin) but no statement, flag `❌ Missing`.

## 6. Data presentation
For all data in a plot/chart/visual, confirm:
1. Individual data points shown when possible, and always for n ≤ 10.
2. Format shows distribution clearly (dot plots, box-and-whisker).
3. Box-plot elements defined (center line/median; box limits/quartiles; whiskers 1.5×IQR; points/outliers).
4. Clearly defined error bars present and what they represent (SD, SE, CI) is noted.
These describe figure rendering, so they are mostly `👤 Author-only` (verify in figures) — but scan figure legends for error-bar definitions and box-plot element definitions, and report anything obviously missing.

## 7. Image integrity
- Confirm the authors have read Nature Portfolio's image-integrity policy and all images comply. `👤 Author-only`, but note the policy: unprocessed data provided upon request; no inappropriate splicing of blots/gels; loading controls on the same blot. If the paper has blots/gels/micrographs, remind the author.

## 8. Additional policy considerations
Prompt: indicate whether each applies. For each, decide **applies: yes/no** from the manuscript, then deep-dive only those that apply.

- **Macromolecular structural data** → **Validation report**: confirm an official wwPDB validation report is provided for all macromolecular structures.
- **Unique biological materials** → **Obtaining biological materials**: either "described restrictions in the manuscript" or "described how to obtain all materials."
- **Research animals and/or animal-derived materials requiring ethical approval** → Ethical compliance statement; Ethics committee (name of board/institution that approved the protocol). ARRIVE guidelines recommended.
- **Human embryos, gametes and/or stem cells** → Ethics statement with: approving committee(s); informed consent from donors/recipients; conditions for donating materials. Confirm policy compliance.
- **Human research participants** → Ethics approval (name of board/institution that approved protocol OR provided guidelines); Informed consent (obtained from all participants, noted in manuscript); Identifiable images (consent to publish obtained and noted in Methods, per participant release form) OR "no identifiable images."
- **Clinical data** → Clinical trial registration (registration number from ClinicalTrials.gov or equivalent); Phase 2/3 RCT → CONSORT checklist provided, OR "not a phase 2/3 RCT"; Tumor marker prognostic studies → REMARK guidelines followed, OR "not a tumor marker prognostic study."
- **Archaeological, geological, and palaeontological materials** → affirm responsible collection/export per permits and local laws, detailed in the manuscript.

Evidence to look for: patient/tissue cohorts ("Human research participants" and possibly "Clinical data"); PDB/EMDB accessions ("Macromolecular structural data"); requests for unique reagents/plasmids/cell lines ("Unique biological materials"); animal work; stem-cell lines; trial registration numbers.

## 9. Certification & signature
- "I certify that all the above information is complete and correct." + **Typed signature** + **Date**. `👤 Author-only` — leave placeholders in the report.
