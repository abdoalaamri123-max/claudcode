# Proofing Checklist (pass by pass)

Read this for the line-by-line check of a page proof. Work one pass at a time, comparing the proof against the final accepted manuscript. Classify each finding principal (must fix before approving) or minor (fix where possible). The rule that overrides everything: eliminate 100% of principal errors. Use this as an actual checklist, ticking each item.

## Pass 1 - Author information (principal)

- Every author name spelled correctly: first name, family name, and middle name where used.
- Author order exactly as agreed.
- Affiliation for each author complete and correct: department, institution, city, country.
- ORCID ID for each author accurate, where applicable.
- Corresponding-author email correct and functional (not just correctly spelled).
- Have each author confirm their own name and affiliation.

## Pass 2 - Text accuracy

- **Spelling convention:** UK vs US English is not mixed. Use the journal's specified variety; if none is specified, pick one and apply it consistently throughout.
- **Homophones and homonyms:** scan for similar-sounding (their/there) and similar-looking (gnome/genome) word errors.
- **Technical terms:** complex terms, chemical compounds, and species names spelled correctly.
- No typographical or spelling errors.
- Grammar and punctuation follow the journal's style.
- Abbreviations and acronyms defined at first use and used consistently thereafter.
- **Alphanumeric symbols (principal for gene/protein names):** check short forms and alphanumeric characters of gene and protein symbols; the letter I and numeral 1 are easily interchanged, e.g. in PI3K. Also O vs 0, l vs 1.
- Correct scientific nomenclature, symbols, and units.

## Pass 3 - Data, numbers, and equations (principal, highest priority)

- Every numerical value matches the original data and the accepted manuscript.
- Decimal places consistent and appropriate.
- Units of measurement correct and consistent across text, tables, and figures.
- Statistical values correct: p-values, confidence intervals, n, SD/SEM, effect sizes, degrees of freedom.
- Equations formatted correctly, with no missing or misplaced symbols, sub/superscripts, or Greek letters.
- **Cross-verify:** compare every value across text, figures, tables, and any dataset; a disagreement is a principal error.
- Read numbers aloud or check columns in reverse to catch transpositions.

## Pass 4 - Figures, tables, and media (principal)

- All figures present and in the correct order.
- Every figure and table is called out in the text, and they appear in sequential order (Fig. 3 cited after Fig. 2, not before).
- Captions, legends, and labels correspond correctly to each figure/table.
- Figure captions match the figures and are accurate.
- Images high-resolution and free of distortion (not squashed, stretched, or cropped by typesetting).
- **Against source files:** where a source figure (TIFF/PNG/JPG) is provided, the proof figure matches it (same image, orientation, cropping, colors, panels); where source data (XLSX/CSV) is provided, every plotted and tabulated value matches the source.
- All panels in multi-part figures labeled correctly (A, B, C, ...).
- Inside figures: numbers, axes, coordinate systems, units, spelling.
- Tables formatted correctly: no missing cells, no misaligned columns; headers and units correct.
- All values in tables match the text, the figures, and the source data.
- **Supplementary videos:** each is the correct clip, called out in the text, with matching legend; metadata (duration, resolution, codec) sensible; format and size meet journal rules.

## Pass 5 - Citations (principal for accuracy)

- All in-text citations match the reference list, one to one.
- Reference list complete and in the journal's format.
- Author names, publication years, journal titles, and DOIs correct.
- No misordering caused by a reference added or deleted late.
- URLs functional and up to date.

## Pass 6 - Formatting and style (minor)

- Headings and subheadings follow journal style.
- Consistent font type, size, and spacing.
- No orphan headings (a heading with no text below it).
- Paragraphs properly aligned and indented.

## Pass 7 - Ethical and legal (principal for compliance)

- Acknowledgements correct.
- Funding sources accurately listed.
- Conflict-of-interest statement included and correct.
- Permissions in place for any reused figures or data.
- Required ethical declarations and financial disclosures present, with the complete information they typically require.
- Approval numbers, and the spelling of approving and funding agencies, checked carefully.
- Disclosed use of AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT) or a language-editing service, per journal policy.

This pass is a **diff against the accepted manuscript**: confirm these declarations survived typesetting correctly and unchanged. Whether they were adequate and complete in the first place (the right approval body named, consent obtained, a deposition actually made) was checked earlier, pre-submission, by `submission-screener`; do not re-derive that judgment here, just confirm nothing was corrupted or dropped in production.

## Before you approve

- Every author query (AQ1, AQ2, ...) answered.
- Every principal error resolved (non-negotiable).
- Corrections marked precisely: exact word/value and its replacement, with page/column/line or figure/table.
- Any genuine scientific error or soundness concern routed to the handling editor by email, not silently changed.
- Corresponding author has circulated the proof so every author confirmed their own details.
